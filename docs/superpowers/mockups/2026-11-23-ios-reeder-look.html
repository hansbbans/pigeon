A · Hairline (recommended)

C · Columns

The densest of the three, closest to Reeder itself. A favicon column down the left, the time aligned right, and the source under the title only where feeds mix. Section heads are grey bands that stick while you scroll. The sidebar is graphite in both light and dark, with each folder's colour as a small square. It shows the most stories per screen, but it is the most list-like, and the nearest of the three to the inbox feel you dislike.
